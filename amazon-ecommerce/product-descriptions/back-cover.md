# From the Back Cover
Writer's Block Can't Kill You—But a Deranged Fan Just Might.

Joscelyn B. Wild (Joss) is a bestselling author who thought her biggest problem was finding inspiration for her next book. But inspiration finds her in the worst possible way when she's abducted by a crazed fan—a wannabe writer with a dangerous obsession and a twisted idea of collaboration.

Trapped in a nightmare straight out of her own novels, Joss is forced to confront her captor's demands, all while trying to outsmart the psycho holding her hostage. This isn't just writer's block; it's a fight for survival, with no guarantee that she'll make it out alive—or sane.

But Joss isn't alone in her struggle. Enter Fury Azazel, a fierce and fiery bounty hunter from the depths of Hell, whose connection to Joss is stronger than her captor realized. As Joss's situation grows more dire, Fury becomes her only hope for survival.

In a deadly game of cat and mouse, Joss must use every ounce of her wit and creativity to escape the clutches of her would-be collaborator. But in a world where the lines between fiction and reality blur, even the most brilliant plot twist might not be enough to save her.

If you thought writer's block was bad, try surviving a fan who wants to write your ending for you.
