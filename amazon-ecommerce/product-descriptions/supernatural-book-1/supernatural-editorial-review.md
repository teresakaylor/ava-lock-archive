# Editorial Reviews
## By Lilian Lilyfield (the Book Revue) ★★★★★

In "Demons Also Dream: Summoned," the first installment of the "Deadly Sins" series, Ava Lock delivers a tantalizing blend of chilling suspense, intricate plots, and a parade of demons that redefine the paranormal fiction genre. This novel weaves a captivating tale where the lines between good and evil blur, leaving readers entranced and yearning for more.

Joss, our protagonist, is a hopeless romantic whose real life falls short of the dark and thrilling romances she craves. Her growing cynicism is palpable as she navigates a world where the bad guys often seem to come out on top. The narrative's tension builds as even the most malevolent villains eventually meet their end, fearing the eternal damnation that awaits them.

Enter Fury, Hell's premier bounty hunter. Tasked with capturing the ghosts of evildoers attempting to escape Death's grasp, Fury is a formidable force armed with fiery powers and accompanied by her loyal helpers. The underworld she traverses is cutthroat, with demons willing to kill for her coveted position. Yet, Fury's journey is as much about spiritual growth as it is about physical battles. The rituals she performs and the celestial beings she encounters test her strength, resolve, and aspirations.

Fury dreams of transcending her demonic origins to serve in Heaven. However, a debt to Lucifer—her former lover—binds her to Hell's relentless grind. This complex relationship adds a tantalizing layer of intrigue and emotional depth to Fury's character, making her both relatable and compelling.

Ava Lock's "Demons Also Dream: Summoned" is more than just a paranormal novel; it is a deep dive into a world where the supernatural intersects with human desires and frailties. Lock's masterful storytelling, rich character development, and imaginative world-building make this book a must-read for fans of the genre. The suspense is relentless, the romance darkly enticing, and the moral complexities thought-provoking.

This first book in the "Deadly Sins" series sets a high bar for what promises to be an epic saga. Fury's quest for redemption and her struggle against both internal and external demons will keep readers glued to the pages. "Demons Also Dream: Summoned" is an enthralling start to a series that explores the very essence of good and evil, love and hate, and the eternal quest for one's true purpose.
