# From the Inside Flap
Fury Azazel doesn't care what label you slap on her—genie, demon, whatever. She's Hell's top bounty hunter, plain and simple. Once, she was stuck granting wishes; now, she's hunting down evil spirits who think they can cheat Death. She's fierce, fiery, and not interested in your feelings.

But lately, the job's been getting tougher, and a string of bad luck has Fury questioning her own edge. With old memories resurfacing and enemies circling, she's got more than just rogue ghosts to worry about. It's a lot to handle, even for someone who's all fury, all the time.

Fury isn't here to play nice, and she's not looking to be a hero. She's just trying to keep the chaos in check without losing herself in the process. But when you're a badass made of fire and rage, that's easier said than done.
