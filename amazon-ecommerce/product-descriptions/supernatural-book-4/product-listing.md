## **Fury Hunts a Ghost at Chocolate World!**

A dark hunger consumes *Demons Rarely Share: Gluttony*, the fourth book in the thrilling supernatural Deadly Sins series. Welcome to Hershey, Pennsylvania, the day after Halloween, where the streets are littered with candy wrappers and haunted by something far more sinister than trick-or-treaters.

**Is it brain worms? Or is it malpractice? Maybe it’s murder!**

Dr. Henry “Munch” Stout, a renowned bariatric surgeon, has been craving more than just chocolate. Possessed by gluttony, he indulges in a macabre feast, seeking out brains to satisfy his insatiable appetite for phenethylamine. Clear your plate for a story that will burrow into your mind and stay with you long after you devour the last page.

### If You Thought the Demons Were Bad, Wait till You Meet the Angels!
While Lucifer is conspiring with cherubs at Stonehenge, Archangel Raphael intervenes at Keystone Medical Center because Dr. Munch isn’t the only one gripped by dark cravings.

His power-hungry nurse, Sharon Metzger, has been orchestrating a horrifying tapeworm diet scheme to keep their patients thin at any cost. Consumed by guilt, the doctor kills the nurse in a fit of rage, but Sharon isn’t the type to accept Death quietly.

Meanwhile, a whole host of new angels appear in Hershey as residents infected by her parasites become obsessed with hoarding food, alcohol, and anything else they can get their hands on, turning them into mindless zombies.

### Hungry for Supernatural Mystery Thrillers? Chocolate? A Side of Sin?
**Everyone’s Favorite Paranormal Bounty Hunter Meets Her Match.**

Join Fury and Beelzebub as they jump into this spine-chilling ghost hunt. Throw in the demon Berith, who can turn any metal into gold but also has a dark hankering for "Beelzer Butter," and you’ve got a recipe for chaos.

Things start off messy when the hunting party pops through a mirror in the famous dark ride and immediately falls into a vat of chocolate. As the paranormal bounty hunters navigate the whimsical backdrop of the chocolate factory, they face:
* Creepy animatronic cows.
* Malfunctioning exhibits.
* Panicked kindergarteners on a field trip.

The baddy hiding inside the dark ride isn’t just out for revenge—Sharon’s a frantic mom-ghost desperately searching for her daughter, Nina. And where is Nina supposed to be? You guessed it: at Chocolate World.

It’s a wild ride filled with spooky twists, bittersweet chaos, and a whole lot of sugary madness.

### Craving Occult Suspense with Psychics?
**Meet Nina Metzger, a gifted psychic dreamer in a pint-sized package.**

Joss gets sidetracked by Nina, a little girl with psychic powers eerily similar to her own. Get this: the kid can astral project and bring people along with her. After immediately bonding with the child, Joss starts questioning her own path, wondering if motherhood could ever fit into her future.

Nina keeps a sketchbook of monsters that she has actually seen—a hard pill for Fury to swallow when those monsters turn out to be real. It’s the ultimate metaphysical mind-bender:
* What came first—the demon or the dreamer?
* Are Joss’s stories real because the demons really exist?
* Or does Fury exist because Joss conjured her up in her imagination?

This chicken-and-egg dilemma sends Fury spiraling into her own existential crisis, leaving both women to grapple with the blurred lines of reality.
